Privacy

The short version: we count visits using privacy-first, cookieless analytics. No cookies, no personal data, no ads, and no following you around the web.

Analytics — what we measure

To understand roughly how many people visit, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics — a privacy-first tool chosen precisely because it doesn't use cookies, doesn't store personal data, and can't identify you or follow you to other sites. It records anonymous, aggregate signals only: page views, the referring site, and a broad region worked out from your request (your IP address is used to derive that, but is never stored).

So this site is not analytics-free, and we would rather say so plainly than imply otherwise: we do count visits. What we don't do is build a picture of you. There is no cross-site tracking and no advertising profile — which is also why there's no cookie-consent banner to click through.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, and we don't track you across the web. No ads, no advertising trackers, no cookies set by us, no accounts and no sign-up.

claudinite.com is a static website. It runs entirely in your browser, with no server of ours behind it — so beyond the anonymous, aggregate analytics described above, there is nothing about you for us to log or keep.

What your browser loads

This site ships no webfonts and no frameworks. It loads exactly one third-party asset: the privacy-first analytics beacon (a small script) from Cloudflare. That service sees your IP address and browser as part of a normal web request, the same as visiting any website.

Links out

Links to GitHub open that site. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies — not this one.
