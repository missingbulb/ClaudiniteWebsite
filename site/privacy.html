Privacy

The short version: no cookies, no tracking, no personal data — just an anonymous count of how many people visit.

claudinite.com is a static website. It runs entirely in your browser. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no server of ours behind it — so there is nothing about you for us to receive, log, or keep.

We don't sell your data, and we don't track you across the web. No ads, no advertising trackers, no cookies set by us. The one thing we measure is anonymous, aggregate visitor numbers.

Analytics

To understand roughly how many people visit, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics — a privacy-first tool chosen precisely because it doesn't use cookies, doesn't store personal data, and can't identify you or follow you to other sites. It records anonymous, aggregate signals only: page views, the referring site, and a broad region worked out from your request (your IP address is used to derive that, but is never stored). No cross-site tracking and no advertising profile — which is also why there's no cookie-consent banner to click through.

What your browser loads

This site ships no webfonts, no frameworks, and no third-party assets beyond one thing: the privacy-first analytics beacon (a small script) from Cloudflare. That service sees your IP address and browser as part of a normal web request, the same as visiting any website.

Links out

Links to GitHub open that site. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies — not this one.
