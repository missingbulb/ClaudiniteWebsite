Mechanism 01

The pack

A convention that ships with its own means of survival.

  • Rules — every session, automatically.
  • Checks — executable: they fail the session, or the PR.
  • Skills — loaded on demand, never carried.
  • Tasks — upkeep, on your repo’s schedule.

FAQ

Fair questions

What gets added to my repo?

A vendored canon, a declaration, hooks, a scheduler workflow and a CI step — one PR.

Will it fight my CLAUDE.md?

No. It adds the layer that file can’t carry, and keeps it lean.

What happens when a check fails?

It holds the session open and states the fix. Repo-facing checks run again on every PR.

Which agents does it support?

Claude Code today. Other agents keep reading your CLAUDE.md.

How do I leave?

Delete .claudinite/, its hook entries, the scheduler workflow and the CI step.

Can it learn my codebase’s own rules?

Yes — from your own merged sessions.
